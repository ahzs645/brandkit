# Province of British Columbia / Supporting pattern studies

New supporting studies informed by the kit’s brown, gold and cream palette, the manuals’ measured layouts and British Columbia’s Pacific dogwood. The floral forms are new geometric drawings, not reproductions of an official emblem. The identifying coat of arms remains complete and upright.

8 patterns, 2 editable applications, a PowerPoint layout and a separate pattern reference sheet. Open pattern-explorer.html offline to adjust scale, density and colour, and download SVG or PNG. patterns-guide.html explains applications.

These additions carry extension `pattern-explorations` in brand.json. The identifying artwork, original application layouts, historical guidance and approval records remain unchanged. Rebuild with the brand's existing generator, or npm run make:patterns.

- **Dogwood repeat**: Complete six-bract flowers repeat in a measured, alternating rhythm. A new geometric interpretation of Pacific dogwood.
- **Petal mosaic**: Single bracts turn through six directions at three scales, with a recurring gold accent.
- **Measured rules**: Fine paired rules and short registration ticks borrow the discipline of the manuals’ measured layouts.
- **Contour bands**: Parallel curves suggest terrain and a shifting coastline without representing a measured map.
- **Rule lattice**: Offset bundles of diagonal rules form a woven field, with one gold course in each repeat.
- **Dogwood bower**: Flowers in several sizes frame an open centre. A focal composition for a cover or invitation.
- **Seed field**: Tiny flowers punctuate an even field of dots. A quiet texture for reverse sides and borders.
- **Stepped courses**: Broad stepped forms alternate direction and scale to make a bold geometric repeat.
