Choose the scale for the surface.

Use small repeats as a texture on backs, borders and large fields. Medium patterns work well in announcement panels. Oversized motifs suit covers and displays. Keep long text on a clear ground, and use the complete identifying logo separately.

Use one pattern family on a surface. Focal compositions work as a single field; repeat presets marked as repeating fields at their supplied tile size. Check a printed proof when producing small details.

Editable starting points.

SVG text fields stay editable. PNGs and PDFs preserve outlined lettering. The presentation layout is also included in the Pattern studies PowerPoint file.
