Over the Edge / Supporting graphics

A pattern of possibilities.

The original O becomes a printed texture, while column rules, halftone dots, torn paper and registration marks draw on the newspaper’s production language. These supporting studies extend the Forest press palette without replacing the masthead.

Additional kit studies. The identifying artwork and existing identity guidance are preserved.

Keep small text and the complete logo on a clear ground. Focal compositions are designed around one centre.

7 starting points

Choose a rhythm.

Explore a preset above, then change its scale, density or colour. Preset SVGs and PNGs are included in the kit.

01 / Repeating field

O, every way

The source O turns through several directions at three sizes. A loose editorial texture for posts, covers and backs.

02 / Repeating field

Press dots

A repeating halftone field swells and falls like ink on newsprint. Dense enough for a bold panel, quiet enough at a smaller scale.

03 / Repeating field

Column rhythm

Column rules, short line lengths and recurring drop-cap Os turn the newspaper grid into a supporting pattern.

04 / Repeating field

Torn edge

Broad paper strips alternate a straight edge with a cut edge. A bold field for covers and presentation panels.

05 / Repeating field

Double impression

A second impression sits just behind the original O. Small registration shifts suggest the physical character of print.

06 / Focal composition

Growth rings

Irregular woodcut contours bring the forest into the print language. A focal composition, rather than a seamless texture.

07 / Repeating field

Proof marks

Registration targets in three sizes repeat in a light field. Occasional O characters connect the production marks to the identity.

Editable applications

Put the pattern to work.

Give the artwork room.

Use small repeats as texture on backs, borders and large fields. Medium patterns work well in announcement panels; oversized motifs suit covers and displays. Keep long text on a clear ground and the complete identifying logo separate from the pattern.

Use one pattern family on a surface. Focal compositions work as a single field. Check a printed proof when producing small details.

Editable SVGs, outlined PNGs and PDFs, a reference sheet and a PowerPoint layout are included in the brand kit.
