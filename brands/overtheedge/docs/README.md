# Over the Edge - brand kit v1.0

UNBC's Independent Student Newspaper. News · Clubs · Events · Calendar.

"Doodle": the paper's own lettering in Cream, Forest, Lichen and Ink, Decalotype headlines (Anton stands in), Helvetica Neue for reading, Salted for the handwritten accents, and the line doodles students leave in their notebooks. It is the identity the paper's own team designed for its posts, stories, slides and calendars.

## Start here

1. Open `index.html` to browse every file offline.
2. Open `guidelines/brand-details.html` for the full reference: voice, logo, colour with contrast, type, icons, doodles, templates and motion.
3. Open `motion/index.html` for the motion library: the logo printing in, loaders and interface motion.
4. Read `guidelines/overtheedge-brand-guide.pdf` for the guide.
5. Use `logos/outlined/overtheedge-horizontal-forest.svg` or the stacked masthead as the primary logo, on Cream (#FFFBF3) or Lichen (#E3EFCF). On Forest, use the Cream logo; on Ink, the Lichen one.
6. Below 288 px wide on screen, or 58 mm in print, use `logos/outlined/words-horizontal-forest.svg` (without the subtext). Below 96 px or 20 mm, use the O (`symbol/`).
7. Install Anton and Salted from `fonts/` and the original Helvetica Neue files from `printer/staff-card/fonts/` before editing live text in the templates.

## Original staff card

The supplied **Staff Card** is also in Templates as a front/back set, with its
original artwork and Helvetica Neue faces. It is the paper's press pass, and
keeps its own colours and typography. Fill the name and position, then use
**Edit & print** to continue in the full Template Printer editor, or download a
**Printer package**. The original portable archive is `templates/staff-card.zip`.
The printer supports people, batch printing, variants, photo fields, barcodes,
lanyards and calibration. Export an edited package and use **Import package into
Over the Edge** to bring it back into this brand.

## What this kit is built from

- **The logo** is the SVG overtheedge.unbc.ca serves in its header, read on 22 October 2026. Its paths are used as they are: OVER, THE and EDGE, the line UNBC'S STUDENT NEWSPAPER and its apostrophe. Nothing is redrawn or reset in a font. The O on its own is the symbol.
- **The site's two-colour original** (forest words, ink subtext) is in `logos/source/`, for the light and dark themes. Every other logo in this kit is one colour.
- **Colour, type, doodles and applications** come from the team's own Doodle identity files, copied small into `reference/identity/` in the repository. The palette slide names Cream #FFFBF3, Lichen #E3EFCF, Forest #155329 and Ink #262626, and the files fill with exactly those. Haze #F5F1EA, Flame #FFF2D8, Crumple #F4F4F4 and Ember #6D4900 are sampled from the posts.
- **Type**: the identity names Decalotype, Helvetica Neue and Salted. Decalotype is a commercial face, so Anton stands in for it. Helvetica Neue and Salted are the supplied originals. Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono, the website's own faces, are kept for the site.
- **The earlier colours** (Pine, Sage, Moss, Kraft, Pulp, Bark and Proof) come from the site's theme and the April 2025 print cover, and stay for the supporting pattern studies and the earlier templates.

## Rules in brief

- One colour per logo: Forest on Cream or Lichen; Lichen on Ink; Cream on Forest.
- Clear space is half the height of the O on every side.
- Four flat colours carry everything: Cream and Lichen for grounds, Forest for the brand, Ink for text. The tints are grounds and doodles, never text.
- Headlines in Decalotype (Anton); reading text and dates in Helvetica Neue; Salted for a word or two at a time (ISSUE, SCHEDULE, DEADLINE, a month), never a paragraph.
- Doodles share one round stroke and no fill but pupils and dots: googly eyes, a calendar with a clock, a flame, a pencil, a paper plane and a spark.
- Around them: thin dashed ink rules, dashed boxes with rounded corners, tilted Forest day tags, blob stickers, a crumpled sheet of paper, zigzag scribbles at the edges, and OVER THE EDGE repeated faintly behind a schedule.
- The Staff Card is the press pass.

## Original typography and wordmarks

Salted and Helvetica Neue use the supplied original font files; their source notices remain with the kit. The website includes a live font tester and font downloads. Salted’s supplied notice is personal use only. Anton remains a stand-in for Decalotype. The stacked masthead retains the subtext beside THE from the supplied September PDF. Wordmark downloads support both horizontal and stacked arrangements.

The logo preview has a Layout dropdown and a Wordmark checkbox for the small UNBC’s Student Newspaper line. Its own colour picker starts in ink, with the main lettering in forest. The checkbox and colour apply to both arrangements and their downloads.
