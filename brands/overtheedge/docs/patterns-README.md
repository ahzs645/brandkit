# Over the Edge / Supporting pattern studies

The original O becomes a printed texture, while column rules, halftone dots, torn paper and registration marks draw on the newspaper’s production language. These supporting studies extend the Forest press palette without replacing the masthead.

7 patterns, 2 editable applications, a PowerPoint layout and a separate pattern reference sheet. Open pattern-explorer.html offline to adjust scale, density and colour, and download SVG or PNG. patterns-guide.html explains applications.

These additions carry extension `pattern-explorations` in brand.json. The identifying artwork, original application layouts, historical guidance and approval records remain unchanged. Rebuild with the brand's existing generator, or npm run make:patterns.

- **O, every way**: The source O turns through several directions at three sizes. A loose editorial texture for posts, covers and backs.
- **Press dots**: A repeating halftone field swells and falls like ink on newsprint. Dense enough for a bold panel, quiet enough at a smaller scale.
- **Column rhythm**: Column rules, short line lengths and recurring drop-cap Os turn the newspaper grid into a supporting pattern.
- **Torn edge**: Broad paper strips alternate a straight edge with a cut edge. A bold field for covers and presentation panels.
- **Double impression**: A second impression sits just behind the original O. Small registration shifts suggest the physical character of print.
- **Growth rings**: Irregular woodcut contours bring the forest into the print language. A focal composition, rather than a seamless texture.
- **Proof marks**: Registration targets in three sizes repeat in a light field. Occasional O characters connect the production marks to the identity.
