Off the press, one word at a time.

Over the Edge moves like a print run: the logo’s words print in and hold, rules roll, pages turn, and the O fills with ink while you wait. These studies cover the logo, loading and the interface. They share a few timings, the site’s own 160 ms and 240 ms among them, and each ends on a still frame you could print.

Rules

How Over the Edge moves

  1. Print, don’t perform.The logo arrives word by word and then holds. It never bounces, spins, blurs, glows or changes colour, and every logo study ends on the exact still logo.
  2. One thing at a time.Stagger related parts by 80 ms, left to right and top to bottom, the order a reader takes in a page.
  3. Loops are for waiting.Only loaders loop, and only the O or a print device loops, never the full logo. Stop the loop as soon as the wait is over.
  4. Still is a valid frame.With reduced motion, show the final frame. Nothing should depend on the animation to be understood.
