# SPARK Lab motion

16 studies derived from the original arrow and spark. Open index.html offline. Replay and pause controls are provided. Reduced motion displays every final frame. SVG studies also support the app’s animation, video and GIF exports.

- **An idea becomes a whole.** Reveal the arrow, spark and original lettering, then hold the complete logo.
- **Use the website’s easing.** Ease-out-expo settles entrances; short, direct transitions acknowledge interaction.
- **Loop only while waiting.** Loading indicators stop when the task finishes. Do not loop the identifying logo in a page header.
- **Respect reduced motion.** Show a clear final frame immediately when reduced motion is requested. Preserve all information and controls.
