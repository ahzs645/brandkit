MOTION RULES

Make every move useful.

  1. An idea becomes a whole.Reveal the arrow, spark and original lettering, then hold the complete logo.
  2. Use the website’s easing.Ease-out-expo settles entrances; short, direct transitions acknowledge interaction.
  3. Loop only while waiting.Loading indicators stop when the task finishes. Do not loop the identifying logo in a page header.
  4. Respect reduced motion.Show a clear final frame immediately when reduced motion is requested. Preserve all information and controls.
