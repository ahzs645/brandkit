WHO WE ARE

A place for
your next idea.

SparkLab is UNBC’s product creation lab — a space where students, researchers, and community members transform concepts into tangible prototypes.

With access to 3D printers, electronics workstations, CAD software, and expert mentorship, we bridge the gap between imagination and innovation.
