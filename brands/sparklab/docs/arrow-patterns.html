12 STARTING POINTS

From a texture to a statement.

All 12 presets are included in the brand kit as vector SVGs and PNGs. Inward rings and Orbit are focal compositions; the others are repeating tiles at their preset settings.

01 / REPEATING TILE

All directions

Eight directions and three arrow sizes share a modular field. A repeat for ideas moving every way.

02 / REPEATING TILE

Loose scatter

Small, medium and large arrows sit at varied angles with generous space between them.

03 / REPEATING TILE

Cross currents

Alternating streams flow in opposite directions. The arrows swell and recede along each wave.

04 / REPEATING TILE

Arrows & sparks

The original arrow and spark trade places in a repeat of different directions and sizes.

05 / FOCAL FIELD

Orbit

Arrows follow three elliptical paths around a spark. A focal composition for a cover or invitation.

06 / REPEATING TILE

Spark constellation

Sparks in four sizes punctuate a field of tiny dots. A quieter alternative to an arrow pattern.

07 / REPEATING TILE

Micro repeat

Small arrows, one steady direction. A quiet texture for wraps, backs and borders.

08 / REPEATING TILE

Staggered rows

A medium-scale repeat with offset rows and a recurring orange beat.

09 / REPEATING TILE

Oversized crops

The same arrow at a much larger scale. Bold enough for a cover or a wall.

10 / REPEATING TILE

Mixed scales

Three sizes share one modular grid. Small ideas build into larger ones.

11 / FOCAL FIELD

Inward rings

Arrows grow as they approach an open centre. A focal composition for invitations and covers.

12 / REPEATING TILE

Meeting points

Four arrows point into each meeting point. Two cluster sizes repeat across the field.
