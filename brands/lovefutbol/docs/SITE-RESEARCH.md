# love.fútbol: deeper website and editorial research

Reviewed 30 October 2026. This extends the initial research with primary-source observations and explicit local application decisions. It is not an organization-issued communications manual. See the [source register](../reference/sources.json), [visual evidence](../reference/site-visual-evidence.json), [content model](../reference/content-patterns.json) and [working application guide](APPLICATION-GUIDE.md).

## Main finding

The useful next addition is an editable communication system: community project stories, evidence cards, audience-specific calls to action, and verified locale labels. The site supports a broader identity than the first Projects-page sample. It also contains uneven translations, historical campaigns, and inconsistent metadata. A reusable kit should capture the consistent community-led position while keeping dates, program names, currencies, and impact figures outside permanent identity tokens.

The site has several overlapping communication layers: the organization; language and regional presence; supporter programs; geographic initiatives and partnerships; individual spaces; and dated campaigns or publications. These are practical editorial categories inferred from the sources, not an official brand architecture.

## 1. Mission, vision, values, and voice

Three official pages express related ideas with different emphasis:

| Source | Verified emphasis | Practical local application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Governance & Transparency](https://www.lovefutbol.org/governance-transparency) | The mission connects community sports spaces to youth development, community participation, and wider social effects. Its vision explicitly includes all generations. | Use for organizational background and institutional documents. Do not imply that only children may use the spaces. |
| [FAQ](https://www.lovefutbol.org/faq) | Partnership with local leaders, inclusive access, community stewardship, and a child-focused vision. Soccer is a starting point for multi-use spaces. | Describe residents as decision-makers and contributors. Do not describe love.fútbol as operating every local league or delivering every activity. |
| [Methodology](https://www.lovefutbol.org/methodology) | Community participation and sustainability run through the project process. Local people are described as “key leaders of the process.” | Project briefs should give community participation and continued care their own fields. Building completion alone is an incomplete story. |

These formulations are complementary, but none should be silently substituted for an exact official mission quotation from another page. The governance webpage lists ten values; its linked culture document includes an additional ownership/commitment principle. A kit can summarize the shared themes without claiming a universal fixed count of values. Sources: [governance](https://www.lovefutbol.org/governance-transparency), [culture PDF](https://www.lovefutbol.org/hubfs/LF%20Organizational%20Culture.pdf?hsLang=en).

**Inferred editorial direction:** write warmly and concretely about shared work, access, connection, and responsibility. Give local contributors agency. Explain what a place enables and how it will be cared for. Use specific supported descriptions instead of automatic claims that every project transforms every outcome. The inference follows the methodology and culture sources; it is a local writing recommendation.

**Local starter description, original paraphrase:** “love.fútbol works alongside communities to shape inclusive places for sport and play. Local leadership, shared design, and continued care help these spaces become places where people meet, participate, and build lasting connections.” This is editable starter copy, not a replacement official mission statement.

| Company or foundation sponsoring a space | The [Projects](https://www.lovefutbol.org/projects) and methodology pages invite project sponsorship. The homepage and program pages are evidence of pathways, not evidence of a fixed donor segmentation model. The categories above are a local abstraction for starter templates. Use official landing pages rather than copying embedded HubSpot, Monday, or Donorbox form URLs into permanent assets; the landing page can retain the current destination and context.

## 3. Project naming and a stronger editable story model

The Projects index groups spaces by country and retains place names such as La Z, Santiaguito II, Teotihuacán II, and Paraisópolis. These are named community places, not standardized product names. Preserve diacritics and meaningful numerals. Keep a facility's verified proper name separate from its localized generic description and geographic fields. Source: [Projects](https://www.lovefutbol.org/projects).

Current profiles can be very sparse. Direct public HTML inspection of [La Z](https://www.lovefutbol.org/mx-la-z) and [Guildford Heights Park](https://www.lovefutbol.org/ca-guildford-heights-park) found a Brazil return-link label even though their context is not Brazil. [Hissey Playground](https://www.lovefutbol.org/usa-hissey-playground) has a Philadelphia location and an “other” return-link label. A copied navigation label is therefore inadequate evidence for country classification. These are source-quality observations, not proposed brand conventions.

The [La Z inauguration article](https://www.lovefutbol.org/news/united-by-the-game-atlas-femenil-and-love.f%C3%BAtbol-inaugurate-the-la-z-pitch) supplies more useful editorial context: the Tabachines neighborhood in Zapopan, Jalisco; Atlas Femenil's involvement; and participation by girls and women. Its article-level project counts belong to that publication date. The reusable project model should support verified place, community, and partner information without making a numbered milestone mandatory.

**Recommended local fields:** proper name; display language; community/neighborhood; city, region, and country; optional initiative or campaign associations; short factual summary; community participation; activities/access; continued care; partner names with their specific contribution; event date; article publication/update dates; source links; and an optional image with alt text, credit, and permission status. A field may remain empty rather than be inferred from a URL, image filename, or news tag.

**Local case-study sequence:** place and purpose → local participation → what changed → how it will remain active → documented evidence → next action. These are proposed sections, not a claim that the organization uses this exact sequence.

## 4. Initiatives, partnerships, campaigns, and time

| Layer | Observed example | Implication for the kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic initiative | [Penrose news](https://www.lovefutbol.org/news/penrose-elementary-school-community-celebrated-the-opening-of-a-new-community-driven-play-space-as-part-of-philly-plays-and-the-international-power-to-play-partnership) describes Philly Plays as a citywide love.fútbol initiative. | Optional initiative field. A city name or initiative label does not create a new core logo. |
| International partnership | The same Penrose story associates the space with Power to Play, involving Wrexham AFC Foundation and love.fútbol in the United States and Wales. | Allow more than one association per project; an initiative and partnership may overlap. |
| Local program/operator context | [Safe-Hub news](https://www.lovefutbol.org/news/safehub) distinguishes the facility, wider campus, supporting organizations, and Safe Play Corridors™. | Record who supports, operates, designs, funds, or maintains a space only when a source establishes that role. |
| Milestone fundraising campaign | [100 Projects](https://100.lovefutbol.org/) centers the 2025 Hissey milestone and commemorative material. | Campaign numbers, pins, and fundraising asks remain dated campaign material. |
| Anniversary campaign | The current [global homepage](https://www.lovefutbol.org/) features twenty-year messaging. | Optional anniversary field with dates; exclude age claims from evergreen boilerplate. |
| Historical apparel collaboration | [Thread The Future](https://www.lovefutbol.org/news/thread-the-future-jersey) concerns a 2022 EA SPORTS/FIFA 23 jersey collaboration. | Campaign graphics do not establish a permanent pattern library, palette, or reuse license. |

The Penrose story's event occurred on July 31, 2026, while the page identifies an August 1 update. The Safe-Hub page carries a January 2026 update but describes a March 2025 event. Thread The Future similarly has a 2026 update attached to 2022 campaign content. Store event date and article update date separately; do not infer a launch year from a migration/update timestamp.

Partner galleries are not an authorization to assemble a new endorsement lockup. Optional text acknowledgments with role and source are more defensible starter content than bundled third-party logos. Publicly visible photography and campaign artwork likewise remain subject to their own permissions.

## 5. English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese

The current global domain offers English, `/es/`, and `/pt-br/` routes. The companion model contains only short observed interface labels and taglines, with their page sources. It preserves the name love.fútbol across locales. It does not invent official translated mission text. Verified label sources: [Spanish Projects](https://www.lovefutbol.org/es/projects), [Brazilian Portuguese Projects](https://www.lovefutbol.org/pt-br/projects), and [English Projects](https://www.lovefutbol.org/projects).

Localization extends beyond literal translation. The Portuguese homepage uses the heading “DO ESPAÇO AO PERTENCIMENTO”; its emphasis on belonging is a useful contextual observation, not a required translation of an English heading. The [regional Brazil](https://www.lovefutbolbrasil.org/) and [LATAM](https://www.lovefutbollatam.org/) sites are explicitly cross-linked regional presences with different regional design/content contexts. They should not override the current global site's palette or counts merely because they remain live.

Practical cautions found in live pages:

- The [Spanish homepage](https://www.lovefutbol.org/es/) shows a 19-dollar monthly message near the hero and 199 MXN lower down. The [Portuguese homepage](https://www.lovefutbol.org/pt-br/) similarly has US$19 and R$20. These may represent distinct regional offers or incomplete editing; the pages do not resolve the relationship. An amount-free default CTA avoids guessing.
- The Spanish annual community-reach figure is 14,200, while the Portuguese page shows 255,200 under a comparable label. Both show 128 projects and 24 countries. These cannot be safely reconciled into one evergreen fact without additional clarification.
- Both localized hero buttons still expose a CMS placeholder linking to HubSpot. Reuse verified donation/contact landing routes rather than these buttons.
- The homepage assigns different project numbers to an El Sarchal image and nearby caption. Optional project numbering requires its own reliable source.

**Local rule of thumb:** locale is a content attribute, not a currency. Currency, donation frequency, target market, and campaign should each be explicit. Translated proper names should be reviewed against the actual place; preserve the source spelling when no verified local form exists.

## 6. Evidence and impact cards

Methodology names three impact areas: Youth Play; Community Engagement and Ownership; Platform for Social Change. It presents questionnaire percentages and cites samples of 184 and 135, but the rendered page does not associate each percentage with a specific study period or subset. Source: [Methodology](https://www.lovefutbol.org/methodology).

The local card model should therefore contain `value: null` by default, not a demonstration number. Add metric definition, unit, geographic/program scope, time period, source, source date, denominator where relevant, method note, and last review date. Where a source is a survey, retain perception or self-report wording. Do not convert an observed association into a causal promise. These are editorial data-quality choices, not love.fútbol's official reporting schema.

A reusable evidence slide can also be qualitative: an attributed community observation, a documented participation process, or a maintenance plan. Leave quote and image permissions as editable evidence fields. No fabricated testimonial is needed to make a starter useful.

## 7. Broader visual evidence and implementation choices

The nine-page visual audit read public HTML and matching CSS, then inspected selected directly linked images and official report pages. It did not measure browser-computed styles or confirm commercial font loading. The [sanitized evidence record](../reference/site-visual-evidence.json) preserves selectors, declarations, page scope and limits.

| Observation | Source context | Consequence for this kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit Bahianita spans, roughly 48–89 px | Home, About, Methodology, Governance, Donate and Contact, in addition to Projects | Broaden the observed display role. The reusable scale remains a local choice. |
| Active cyan `#3CB1EA` in headings, counters, bands and calls to action | [Home](https://www.lovefutbol.org/) and [About/Partners](https://www.lovefutbol.org/about-us#partners) | Add an observed cyan accent. Use charcoal on it for 6.97:1 contrast; white is only 2.42:1. |
| Plain project-title H1 elements match the brush-font rule | [Dharavi](https://www.lovefutbol.org/dharavi-india), [Milpa Alta](https://www.lovefutbol.org/milpaalta), shared child CSS | Record a real cascade match for DK Brush Crush, while keeping the font-load limitation explicit. |
| Landscape 16:9 gallery and square detail thumbnails | Homepage and detail-page gallery modules | Use editable image frames with captions. Cropping a thumbnail does not change the stored photograph. |
| Homepage counters use four, two and one columns; photo gallery moves from three to one | Matching module CSS, breakpoints recorded in the evidence JSON | Use stacking and natural wrapping as local responsive principles; do not label module-specific breakpoints a universal brand grid. |
| Partner strips fit marks within maximum boxes and wrap | Home strip and About galleries | Use separate attribution areas and optical balance; no fixed official co-branding ratio follows from these CSS boxes. |
| Flat bands, subtle black/blue grain fields, rectangular images and fine separators | Live pages; selected 2025 report interiors | Add a flat charcoal community-voice layout with light-green display type. The kit does not fabricate an official pattern or icon family from these examples. |

The complete official website raster is 1275 × 875 px. Its nontransparent artwork bounds are `[205,172,1137,704]`; the smallest transparent inset is 138 px, about 0.259 of the visible 532 px height. This supports a practical **0.25H external reserve**, but raster padding is not a published clear-space rule. The site's 121 px header logo CSS applies to the padded image canvas, not 121 px of visible artwork.

The implemented working recommendations are **32 mm print / 144 CSS px screen**, with a proof at final output size. They are conservative local choices informed by a rendered size study, not dimensions recovered from a manual. The existing 48 mm report logo and 8 mm document reserve already exceed the proposed quarter-height reserve. See the [application guide](APPLICATION-GUIDE.md) for the complete reasoning and specimens.

Scoped brand-manual searches and explicit resource links did not yield a public visual standards manual. The Brazilian [methodology page](https://www.lovefutbolbrasil.org/metodologia-2) mentions an operational project manual; its purpose does not establish graphic standards. That unsuccessful search is a research boundary, not proof that internal guidance does not exist.

## 8. How the findings are applied

1. **Editable content presets:** project profile, community participation story, evidence card, and audience-specific CTA. Keep blanks for unverified facts.
2. **Verified locale labels:** English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese interface labels with fixed love.fútbol naming; make longer copy explicitly editable/local.
3. **Assumption register:** record each practical choice, supporting source IDs, confidence, and what remains unverified. Include amount-free CTAs, explicit partner roles, separate event/update dates, and source-linked metrics.
4. **Template refinements:** add community role and continued care fields to a project brief; give evidence slides source/period space; use cyan as an optional observed accent where contrast is suitable. No need to insert a real campaign or partner logo into starter templates.
5. **Context notes:** distinguish organization, regional presence, supporter program, initiative, partnership, project, and dated campaign. This helps future contributors avoid treating every new campaign design as a rebrand.

The follow-up implementation adds six layouts: project profiles in Letter and A4, comparison and community-voice slides, a metric card and a call-to-action card. Four new guide pages explain working dimensions, project content, evidence and audiences. The machine-readable assumptions remain separate from the unresolved official rules.
