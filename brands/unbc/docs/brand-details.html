UNBC identity and document applications

Official full-logo artwork and published identity rules, with editable department names and local report, brief, portfolio and presentation layouts.

Source guidance February 2020 · Kit applications 1.0 · Sources retrieved 2026-10-26

Logo sizing and colour

38.1 mm minimum width

Measure the visible full logo. Keep its original 434.89:74.97 proportions.

Clear space on every side

Reserve the gap between the top of B and top of N. For this source artwork that is conservatively 0.03691 × logo width. At 55 mm width, reserve at least 2.03 mm; document layouts reserve 8 mm.

Green white and black

Green #035642 on white. Black for black-only reproduction. White preferably on UNBC Green. Gold #B79527 is an accent and is never used for the logo.

Department sub-logos

A unit’s sub-logo is the full logo with its name set under NORTHERN BRITISH COLUMBIA in Helvetica Neue 95 Black, at the position, size and leading measured from UNBC’s own sub-logos. Long names wrap, with “and” and “&” opening a line. The kit includes 88 units’ sub-logos; the Departments view sets any other name the same way.

In documents, the department (12 pt bold) and subdepartment (10.5 pt regular) are ordinary text below the logo. These sizes are local document defaults.

Typography and sources

Helvetica Neue is official; Arial is the documented alternative. Office files request Arial. TeX Gyre Heros is bundled only for open portable rendering, with its licence and substitute notice.

Original vector coordinates are retained. Generated green follows the manual’s #035642 recipe rather than the website SVG’s #00573D. Gold uses the manual’s RGB 183,149,39, consistent with #B79527. Source bytes, transformations and hashes are recorded in PROVENANCE.json; rules and local defaults in identity-rules.json.
