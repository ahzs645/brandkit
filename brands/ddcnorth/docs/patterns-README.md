# ddcnorth / Supporting pattern studies

The six-dot identity becomes an index, a field and a network. Complete six-dot groups carry the recognizable shape, while individual circles supply quieter textures. These supporting studies extend the palette and geometry already used by ddcnorth; they do not change the approved identifying artwork.

8 patterns, 2 editable applications, a PowerPoint layout and a separate pattern reference sheet. Open pattern-explorer.html offline to adjust scale, density and colour, and download SVG or PNG. patterns-guide.html explains applications.

These additions carry extension `pattern-explorations` in brand.json. The identifying artwork, original application layouts, historical guidance and approval records remain unchanged. Rebuild with the brand's existing generator, or npm run make:patterns.

- **Micro index**: Small, complete six-dot groups repeat on a tight registration grid. A restrained texture for the back of a card or a border.
- **Turning groups**: Three sizes and four quarter-turns share a modular field. The repeat changes direction while every six-dot group stays intact.
- **Resolution**: Individual circles grow and shrink through a repeating wave. A quiet field informed by data density, without implying measured values.
- **Step repeat**: Medium six-dot groups alternate orientation and rhythm. A bolder repeat for covers, wrapping and presentation dividers.
- **Connection lanes**: Six-dot groups meet along stepped paths. Solid junctions and a single bright route give a network field a clear rhythm.
- **Convergence**: Small and large six-dot groups gather around a central group. A focal composition for an invitation, chapter break or presentation.
- **Circle weave**: Three circle sizes interleave in diagonal runs. A repeating texture drawn from the identity’s simplest unit.
- **Scale contrast**: One large six-dot group balances a dense micro field. Use the open middle as a visual pause rather than a text container.
