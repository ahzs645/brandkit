Motion · an example for the 1983 identity

Power that arrives on time.

Seven studies for the 1983 signature: three for the logo, two loaders made of the symbol alone, and two for interfaces. They share four timings and two eases, and each ends on the signature exactly as the manual draws it.

The 1983 Graphic Standards Manual predates screen motion and says nothing about it. These studies are brandkit’s example of how the identity could move under the manual’s rules; they are not BC Hydro guidance.

Rules

How B.C.Hydro moves

  1. The signature arrives whole.Symbol and logotype are inseparable in the manual, so every logo study ends on the complete signature, its positions and proportions unchanged.
  2. Colour stays in the symbol.Green and blue move only inside the ring; the logotype stays black. No other colours, no glows, no gradients.
  3. Loops are for waiting.Only the symbol loops, never the full signature, and only while something loads.
  4. Still is a valid frame.With reduced motion, every study shows its final frame. Nothing depends on the animation to be understood.
