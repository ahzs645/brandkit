B.C.Hydro / Supporting graphics

A pattern of possibilities.

New supporting studies informed by the 1983 system’s circular construction, green and blue, and the movement of water and energy. These abstract graphics are contemporary kit explorations, not patterns prescribed by the historical manual. Keep the official signature complete and separate from the patterned field.

Additional kit studies. The identifying artwork and existing identity guidance are preserved.

Keep small text and the complete logo on a clear ground. Focal compositions are designed around one centre.

6 starting points

Choose a rhythm.

Explore a preset above, then change its scale, density or colour. Preset SVGs and PNGs are included in the kit.

01 / Repeating field

Ring cadence

An abstract annulus repeats at three sizes. Paired rings establish a measured rhythm without using the identifying Hydro symbol.

02 / Repeating field

Current bands

Three parallel strands travel through broad, alternating waves. A strong field for covers and large announcement panels.

03 / Repeating field

Transmission

Orthogonal routes meet circular junctions in a repeating grid. An abstract supporting diagram for systems and project stories.

04 / Focal composition

Radial pulse

Short radial strokes and independent rings concentrate around an open centre. A focal graphic for title pages and presentation covers.

05 / Repeating field

Phase steps

Stepped channels form a dense, measured repeat. Use it at a larger scale for a bolder alternative to flowing curves.

06 / Focal composition

Reservoir contours

Nested organic contours suggest water and terrain. A quiet focal composition for reports, dividers and technical stories.

Editable applications

Put the pattern to work.

Give the artwork room.

Use small repeats as texture on backs, borders and large fields. Medium patterns work well in announcement panels; oversized motifs suit covers and displays. Keep long text on a clear ground and the complete identifying logo separate from the pattern.

Use one pattern family on a surface. Focal compositions work as a single field. Check a printed proof when producing small details.

Editable SVGs, outlined PNGs and PDFs, a reference sheet and a PowerPoint layout are included in the brand kit.
