# B.C.Hydro: Graphic Standards Manual, 1983 (reconstruction)

**A historical reconstruction for reference and study. This is not current BC Hydro brand
guidance.**

BC Hydro’s Graphic Standards Manual of June 1983 is rebuilt page by page in brandkit’s manual
library (`manuals/bc`, book “hydro”). This kit is generated from `source/brand.json`, which turns
its rules into data.

## What is here

- **The four signatures**, each in every permitted form:
  - Corporate
  - Gas Operations
  - B.C.Hydro Rail
  - Formal

  The forms are corporate colours, black, white, the audio-visual corporate reverse, and blind
  embossed (for business cards).
- **Stationery** for the Corporate, Gas Operations and Rail sets: letterhead, No. 10 envelope and
  business card (pp. 14–17).
- **A form** on the forms grid (p. 19).
- **Publications** (pp. 23–29): report, illustrated and display covers, and two mastheads.
- **Advertising** (pp. 31–33), an **audio-visual** title frame (p. 34), **signs** (pp. 37–40) and
  **vehicles** (pp. 41–43).
- **A guide**, generated from the same definition, including the backgrounds-and-value rules,
  every incorrect usage, and the minimum-size table.

## What is approximated

- **Signature artwork.** The signatures are PofBC’s reconstruction: the symbol drawn from the
  manual, the logotype fitted letter by letter in TeX Gyre Termes.
- **Typeface.** Times Roman is set throughout in TeX Gyre Termes, an open-licence equivalent
  (GUST Font License, `fonts/`).
- **Colour.** Corporate green (PMS 376) and blue (PMS 314) are screen approximations; the
  manual’s process recipes are in `guidelines/COLOURS.json`.
- **Missing artwork.** The B.C.Hydro Rail “BCE route” emblem is not included.
- **Undimensioned items.** Where the manual gives no measurements (vehicles, some signs),
  proportions come from its drawings, as each template’s description says.
