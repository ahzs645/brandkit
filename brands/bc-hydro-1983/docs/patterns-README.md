# B.C.Hydro / Supporting pattern studies

New supporting studies informed by the 1983 system’s circular construction, green and blue, and the movement of water and energy. These abstract graphics are contemporary kit explorations, not patterns prescribed by the historical manual. Keep the official signature complete and separate from the patterned field.

6 patterns, 2 editable applications, a PowerPoint layout and a separate pattern reference sheet. Open pattern-explorer.html offline to adjust scale, density and colour, and download SVG or PNG. patterns-guide.html explains applications.

These additions carry extension `pattern-explorations` in brand.json. The identifying artwork, original application layouts, historical guidance and approval records remain unchanged. Rebuild with the brand's existing generator, or npm run make:patterns.

- **Ring cadence**: An abstract annulus repeats at three sizes. Paired rings establish a measured rhythm without using the identifying Hydro symbol.
- **Current bands**: Three parallel strands travel through broad, alternating waves. A strong field for covers and large announcement panels.
- **Transmission**: Orthogonal routes meet circular junctions in a repeating grid. An abstract supporting diagram for systems and project stories.
- **Radial pulse**: Short radial strokes and independent rings concentrate around an open centre. A focal graphic for title pages and presentation covers.
- **Phase steps**: Stepped channels form a dense, measured repeat. Use it at a larger scale for a bolder alternative to flowing curves.
- **Reservoir contours**: Nested organic contours suggest water and terrain. A quiet focal composition for reports, dividers and technical stories.
