A pattern of possibilities.

Northwind’s seven-dot geometry gives the patterns a compact, rounded rhythm. Turning clusters, drifting dots and contour bands suggest movement without introducing arrows or changing the identifying mark.

Additional kit studies. The identifying artwork and existing identity guidance are preserved.

Keep small text and the complete logo on a clear ground. Focal compositions are designed around one centre.

01 / Repeating field

Turning field

Intact seven-dot clusters turn through four visible orientations at three sizes. A modular repeat with a changing direction.

02 / Repeating field

North current

Clusters travel in alternating streams, swelling and receding as they pass. A calm directional repeat for larger surfaces.

03 / Repeating field

Drift lines

Individual circles follow gentle, interleaved waves. A light texture for a cover margin, document back or digital panel.

04 / Repeating field

Ring lattice

Hollow circles keep the original seven-dot spacing while opening the pattern. Alternating turns form an airy lattice.

05 / Repeating field

Overscale

Large clusters meet the edge of the tile. Broad, rounded forms suit covers, wraps and environmental graphics.

06 / Focal composition

Wind rose

Seven-dot clusters circulate around a clear centre, with smaller dots extending the rhythm. A focal composition rather than a repeat.

07 / Focal composition

Dot canopy

A field of changing circle sizes opens into a soft central clearing. A supporting texture for a quiet title or a photograph.

08 / Repeating field

Seven grid

Small clusters keep one direction and one scale. A restrained repeat for borders, stationery and reverse sides.

Editable applications

Put the pattern to work.

Give the artwork room.

Use small repeats as texture on backs, borders and large fields. Medium patterns work well in announcement panels; oversized motifs suit covers and displays. Keep long text on a clear ground and the complete identifying logo separate from the pattern.

Use one pattern family on a surface. Focal compositions work as a single field. Check a printed proof when producing small details.

Editable SVGs, outlined PNGs and PDFs, a reference sheet and a PowerPoint layout are included in the brand kit.
