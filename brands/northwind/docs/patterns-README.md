# northwind / Supporting pattern studies

Northwind’s seven-dot geometry gives the patterns a compact, rounded rhythm. Turning clusters, drifting dots and contour bands suggest movement without introducing arrows or changing the identifying mark.

8 patterns, 2 editable applications, a PowerPoint layout and a separate pattern reference sheet. Open pattern-explorer.html offline to adjust scale, density and colour, and download SVG or PNG. patterns-guide.html explains applications.

These additions carry extension `pattern-explorations` in brand.json. The identifying artwork, original application layouts, historical guidance and approval records remain unchanged. Rebuild with the brand's existing generator, or npm run make:patterns.

- **Turning field**: Intact seven-dot clusters turn through four visible orientations at three sizes. A modular repeat with a changing direction.
- **North current**: Clusters travel in alternating streams, swelling and receding as they pass. A calm directional repeat for larger surfaces.
- **Drift lines**: Individual circles follow gentle, interleaved waves. A light texture for a cover margin, document back or digital panel.
- **Ring lattice**: Hollow circles keep the original seven-dot spacing while opening the pattern. Alternating turns form an airy lattice.
- **Overscale**: Large clusters meet the edge of the tile. Broad, rounded forms suit covers, wraps and environmental graphics.
- **Wind rose**: Seven-dot clusters circulate around a clear centre, with smaller dots extending the rhythm. A focal composition rather than a repeat.
- **Dot canopy**: A field of changing circle sizes opens into a soft central clearing. A supporting texture for a quiet title or a photograph.
- **Seven grid**: Small clusters keep one direction and one scale. A restrained repeat for borders, stationery and reverse sides.
