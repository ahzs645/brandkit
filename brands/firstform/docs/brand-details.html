Positioning

Broad on purpose.

First Form takes on websites, forms, products and processes. The brand describes the outcome, a first working version, so it never has to be rewritten when the work changes.

From first idea to first form.

What we do

Consulting, design and development. We scope the problem, shape it and build the first version that works.

Who it's for

Founders, teams and researchers with an idea that needs to become something people can use or test.

Descriptor

Use one everywhere: Consulting · Design · Development. Add a specific service only in context.

Voice: say
  • Plain verbs: scope, design, build, test.
  • The real thing: "the probe holder", "the intake form".
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • "We" for the practice, "you" for the client.
Voice: avoid
  • Lists of every service in one line.
  • Hype words: revolutionary, world-class, cutting-edge.
  • Jargon a client would have to look up.
  • "Under construction" or apologies on live pages.

Two blocks, one form.

Color

Strictly monochrome.

Black, white and grey. Contrast does the work color usually does, and real photos of the work bring the color. Select any swatch to copy its hex.

Support greys

Proportion

Dark grounds lead (50). Ash (20) and White (15) frame content and the logo. Graphite (10) separates. Pure Black (5) is for buttons and emphasis. The Shadow Gradient (#060606 → #404040, 120°) is for social posts and title slides only, never behind the logo.

Patterns

Built from the mark's geometry.

Each pattern comes from something already in the brand: the isometric angle of the blocks, the module grid, the overlap and the viewfinder. Use them as quiet grounds behind type, at low contrast, never behind the logo.

Interface

Soft corners, hard contrast.

Panels use a 24 px radius, fields 16 px, actions are full pills. Forms sit on Ash so fields read as White wells. This is the pattern for intake, booking and contact forms. Try it below.

Templates

Ready to fill in.
