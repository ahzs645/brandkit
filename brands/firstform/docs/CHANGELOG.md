# Change log

## 1.0 - 22 October 2026

- Added First Form to brandkit, from First Form Brand Kit V1.0.
- Rebuilt the logo as vectors: the two-block mark redrawn on its isometric geometry (150 × 215 units, 3 : 5 slope), and the FIRST / FORM wordmark set in Sora Bold on the original lockup's proportions.
- Kept the kit's palette (Black, Graphite, Ash, White) and added five named support greys: Ink, Coal, Rule, Steel and Mist.
- Set Sora Light, Regular, SemiBold and Bold from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
- Twelve templates: business card front and back, part tag, A4 letterhead and proposal cover, two social posts, social banner, link preview image, and title, case study and closing slides.
- Eight icons: the kit's consultation, design and prototyping, plus website, forms, build, systems and research.
- An eight-page guide, a logo reference sheet and a brand details page (`guidelines/brand-details.html`).
- A redesigned website for First Form in `site/`, with desktop and mobile screenshots.
