Motion · Version 1.0

Parts that fit into one whole.

First Form moves the way its mark is built: two blocks that slide along one axis and lock together. These sixteen studies cover the logo, loading and the interface. Each runs on the same few timings and easings, and each ends on a still frame you could print.

--ff-base320 ms
--ff-slow640 ms
--ff-draw1200 ms
--ff-loop1200 ms
--ff-stagger80 ms
--ff-settlecubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)
--ff-movecubic-bezier(.6, 0, .2, 1)
--ff-linearlinear

The same values are in motion.css as custom properties and in motion-tokens.json for code.

Rules

How First Form moves

  1. Assemble, don’t decorate.Motion shows parts fitting together. Every logo animation ends on the exact still logo: no bounce, blur, glow or colour change.
  2. One thing at a time.Stagger related parts by 80 ms. Move along the mark’s own axes: vertical, or the 3 : 5 isometric slope.
  3. Loops are for waiting.Only loaders loop, and only the mark loops, never the full logo. Stop the loop as soon as the wait is over.
  4. Still is a valid frame.With reduced motion, show the final frame. Nothing should depend on the animation to be understood.
