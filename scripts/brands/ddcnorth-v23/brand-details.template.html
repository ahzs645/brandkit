The six-dot symbol reads the same way: three dots of data, two of design, one connection.

02 · Pattern

Pattern generator

Six pattern families, all built on the symbol’s 88-unit grid. Pick one, change the colourway and density, then copy the SVG straight into Figma, Keynote or a web page.

Colourway

Rules that keep these on-brand: a complete symbol always keeps one dot diameter of empty space around it, so grids and paths stop short of it; only repeated or cropped dots may touch other shapes. Dots are always circles, never squares; the grid spacing always comes from the symbol (dot diameter is 80 of every 88 units); and a pattern never sits behind the full logo lockup. The kit’s own guide already allows repeating and cropping the motif, so these extend that page.

03 · Data

A data language

You work with data, so the brand should chart well. This is a colour order, a numeral style and a chart form that only DDCNorth would use.

UseRule
One seriesMidnight marks on Paper. Graphite for axes and labels in Space Mono.
HighlightMidnight for the point that matters, Ice for everything else.
CategoriesMidnight, Graphite, Ink, in that order. Three is the limit; group the rest. Each has at least 3:1 contrast on Paper.
IceContext only: unlit dots, ranges, backgrounds. Ice marks always carry a Midnight outline, because Ice alone is too faint on Paper.
Big numbersInter Display 500 for running copy; dot-matrix numerals only for one hero figure.

04 · Templates

Templates the kit doesn’t have yet

The kit covers stationery, signage and vehicles. These cover the work itself: findings, proposals and workshops.

05 · Motion

Three moves

Short, quiet and reusable in video, on the site and in slides. Motion lives in fields, paths and loaders; the logo and the complete symbol never move, fade or pulse. Each one respects reduced-motion settings.
